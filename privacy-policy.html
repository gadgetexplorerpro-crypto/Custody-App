1. Overview & Zero-Trust Architecture

CustodyLog (com.aistudio.custodylog.vqrkmp) is a co-parenting and child custody management application for parents in the United States and United Kingdom. Each parent signs in with a separate account via Google Sign-In. Private preferences are isolated strictly to your User ID (UID), and shared family records are accessible only to the two parent accounts linked via an explicit invitation token (CL-XXXXXXXX).

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Identity: Google Sign-In UID, display name, and email address used exclusively for authentication and workspace security.
  • Co-Parenting & Custody Records: Child profile names/DOBs, parenting schedules (2-2-3, 2-2-5-5, Alternating Weekends, Custom), timestamped exchange check-ins, incident descriptions, shared expenses, and co-parent chat messages.
  • Attachments & File Hashes: Photos and PDF documents selected via the zero-permission Android Photo Picker or system document picker, along with on-device SHA-256 hashes.
  • Optional AI Calm Tone Assistant: When you explicitly tap "Rewrite in Calm, Child-Focused Tone", your draft message text is transmitted over HTTPS to generate a neutral BIFF suggestion. Suggestions are never auto-sent.

3. Data Security & Sharing

All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest in Google Cloud / Firebase Firestore. We never sell personal or family data to third parties or advertisers. Subscription payments are handled directly by Google Play Billing.

4. Data Portability & Account Deletion

You can export your records to PDF or CSV at any time in the app, and you can permanently delete your account and personal data either directly inside the app (Settings → Delete Account & Personal Data) or via our Account & Data Deletion Page.
