# CustodyLog — Delete Account & Personal Data

**Application Name:** CustodyLog (`com.aistudio.custodylog.vqrkmp`)  
**Developer / Data Controller Contact:** `privacy@custodylog.app`

This page provides instructions and direct links for deleting your **CustodyLog** account and associated personal data, in compliance with Google Play's User Data & Account Deletion requirements, UK GDPR, and US privacy laws (CCPA/CPRA).

---

## Option 1: Instant In-App Account & Data Deletion (Fastest)

If you have the CustodyLog app installed:

1. Open **CustodyLog**.
2. Tap the **Settings (Gear Icon)** in the top navigation bar (or tap **Delete Account & Data** directly at the bottom of the **Login** or **Home** screen).
3. Scroll to **Legal, Privacy Policy, Terms & Account Deletion** and tap **Delete Account & Personal Data**.
4. Check the confirmation box (*"I understand that deleting my account and personal data is permanent and cannot be undone"*).
5. Tap **Permanently Delete Account & Data Now**.

---

## Option 2: Delete Account & Data Without Opening the App (Web / Email Request)

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot sign in, you can request full account and personal data deletion by sending an email request:

- **Deletion Request Email:** [mailto:privacy@custodylog.app?subject=CustodyLog%20Account%20and%20Data%20Deletion%20Request](mailto:privacy@custodylog.app?subject=CustodyLog%20Account%20and%20Data%20Deletion%20Request)
- **What to include:** The Google Account email address you used to sign in to CustodyLog.
- **Processing Time:** Requests submitted via email are processed and permanently completed within **7 business days** (and always within the statutory 30-day maximum).

## Summary of Data Deleted vs. Retained

| Data Category | Action Upon Account Deletion | Retention Period After Deletion |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **User Profile (`users/{uid}`)** (Name, email, preferences, subscription status) | **Permanently Deleted Immediately** | `0 days` (Deleted immediately) |
| **Firebase Authentication Record** (Google Sign-In UID & email link) | **Permanently Deleted Immediately** | `0 days` (Deleted immediately) |
| **Co-Parent Invitations (`invitations`)** created by your account | **Permanently Deleted Immediately** | `0 days` (Deleted immediately) |
| **Child Profiles (`children`)** created solely by your account | **Permanently Deleted Immediately** | `0 days` (Deleted immediately) |
| **Custody Schedule Events (`schedule_events`)** created by your account | **Permanently Deleted Immediately** | `0 days` (Deleted immediately) |
| **Mutually Shared Co-Parent Audit Logs** (if connected to a second co-parent) | Your personal account profile and login are removed; shared co-parent audit entries remain accessible only to the connected co-parent so neither parent can unilaterally destroy shared financial/exchange records. | Retained for the connected co-parent's workspace until both parents delete the workspace. |
