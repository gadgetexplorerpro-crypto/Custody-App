## 3. Evidentiary Neutrality & Verification Badges

CustodyLog is designed to maintain neutral, factual documentation:
- **Scheduled Event:** A calendar or exchange block scheduled in advance.
- **User-Reported (Unconfirmed):** An exchange check-in, lateness log, or incident documented by one parent that has not yet been confirmed by the other parent. **CustodyLog never treats an unconfirmed user report as an established fact.**
- **Mutually Confirmed:** An event, schedule change, or exchange record verified by both connected parents.
- **Disputed Record:** A record where the co-parent has recorded a neutral disagreement note.

---

## 4. Tamper-Evident Audit Trail

To protect both parents against silent record manipulation:
- **Incidents:** Amendments increment the revision counter (`v1`, `v2`, etc.) and preserve the chronological audit trail and original SHA-256 attachment hash.
- **Messages:** Editing or retracting a sent message preserves the `originalBody` and records an audit note in the communication transcript.
