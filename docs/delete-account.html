Option 1: Instant In-App Account & Data Deletion

  1. Open the CustodyLog Android app.
  2. Tap the Settings (Gear Icon) in the top bar (or tap Delete Account & Data on the Login or Home screen).
  3. Tap Delete Account & Personal Data.
  4. Check the confirmation box and tap Permanently Delete Account & Data Now.
