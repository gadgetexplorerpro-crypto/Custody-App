1. Non-Legal Advice Disclaimer

CustodyLog is a personal scheduling, recordkeeping, expense-tracking, and co-parenting communication software platform. CustodyLog is not a law firm, court agency, or legal representative, and does not provide legal advice or certified court transcripts.

2. Verification Badges & Neutral Recordkeeping

Every record clearly distinguishes between Scheduled Events, User-Reported (Unconfirmed) entries, Mutually Confirmed events, and Disputed records. Unconfirmed reports logged by one parent are never treated as established fact.

3. Tamper-Evident Audit History

Edits to incidents increment revision numbers (v1, v2...) and message edits/retractions preserve the original text in the audit log so neither co-parent can silently alter historical communications.

4. Subscriptions & Google Play Billing

Premium ($12.99/month) and Family Premium ($19.99/month) subscriptions are billed via Google Play and can be managed or canceled anytime in Google Play Subscriptions.

5. Account Deletion
